Memorizing 1/2 to 1/12 Eliminates Long Decimal Multiplication

1/2 മുതൽ 1/12 വരെയുള്ള വിലകൾ 1/4=25% · 1/5=20% · 1/6=16⅔% · 1/8=12.5% · 1/9=11.11% · 1/11=9.09%
• 1/2 = 50% | 1/3 = 33⅓% | 1/4 = 25% | 1/5 = 20% • 1/6 = 16⅔% | 1/7 = 14²⁄₇% | 1/8 = 12.5% • 1/9 = 11.11% | 1/10 = 10% | 1/11 = 9.09% | 1/12 = 8⅓% | 3/8 = 37.5%
Essential Mental Benchmarks: 1/3 = 33.33%, 1/6 = 16.67%, 1/8 = 12.5% (so 3/8 = 37.5%, 5/8 = 62.5%), 1/9 = 11.11%, 1/11 = 9.09%. Instant recall cuts calculation time by 70%!
