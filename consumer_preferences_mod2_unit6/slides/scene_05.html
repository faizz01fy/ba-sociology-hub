The Axiom of Completeness: Decisiveness in Choice

Eliminating Indecision: Every Consumption Bundle Can Be Compared and Ranked
Core Subject Takeaway

Given any two bundles A and B, a consumer can state whether A is preferred to B, B is preferred to A, or both are equally preferred.

Essential Academic Concepts
A > B (Strict Preference) Bundle A yields higher satisfaction than Bundle B.
A = B (Indifference) Both bundles provide the exact same level of utility.
