CORE CONCEPTUAL FOCUS

Capping Quantities Sold to Protect Domestic Markets

Quotas limit the physical volume of a good that may be sold, most commonly applied to foreign imports to protect domestic producers from global competition.

Import Quota
A quantitative limit on the physical volume of imported goods permitted into a nation
Supply Rotation
Aggregate market supply rotates inward from domestic plus foreign supply toward domestic supply alone
Binding vs Non-Binding
A quota is only binding if set below the volume of imports that would freely enter the market
TRADE REGULATION Import Quota vs Open Market
Free Trade Aggregate Supply (S)
Horizontal sum: Domestic Supply + Global Foreign Supply
Abundant Supply · Low Price
Binding Import Quota (Q < Imports)
Total supply curve rotates inward toward domestic supply curve
Higher Price · Lower Quantity
Non-Binding Quota (Q > Imports)
Quota set higher than free market import volumes
Zero Market Impact
Primary Application: Nations deploy import quotas to shield uncompetitive domestic industries from foreign efficiency.
