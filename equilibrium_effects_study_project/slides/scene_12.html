CORE CONCEPTUAL FOCUS

Minimum Wage Laws and Persistent Labor Surpluses

A minimum wage is a price floor. When binding (w > w*), the quantity of labor supplied exceeds labor demanded, generating a persistent labor surplus called unemployment.

Price Floor in Labor
Statutory minimum wage forbidding employers from paying below legal rate w
Labor Surplus (Unemployment)
Binding floor where job seekers (labor supplied) exceed available positions (labor demanded)
Historical Milestones
New Zealand (1894), UK (1909), US Fair Labor Standards Act of 1938 (25¢/hr), $7.25 federal in 2009
