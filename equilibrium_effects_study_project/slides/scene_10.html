Non-Price Rationing

Arbitrary Seller Criteria

  • First-Come, First-Served: Waiting in multi-hour lines
  • Favoritism: Preferential sales to friends & regulars
  • Discrimination: By race, gender, age, or religion
  • Volume Caps: Limiting each buyer to a few gallons
Suppresses price, but maximizes human frustration and friction.
Black Market Evasion

Illegal Market Clearing

  • Side Payments: Paying double the legal price secretly
  • Tie-In Sales: Forcing purchase of overpriced goods
  • City Disparities: No lines in 52 of 92 US cities due to local evasion
Market forces assert themselves around legal obstacles.
The Equilibrium Paradox: It is an equilibrium because neither buyers nor sellers can legally change price under the law.
