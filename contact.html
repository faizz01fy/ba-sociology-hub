Our Fact-Checking & Errata Policy

Academic integrity and conceptual accuracy are fundamental to Academic Hub. When a student or educator flags a potential error—whether in an MCQ key, Malayalam grammatical translation, or curriculum reference—our editorial process follows a strict 3-step SLA:

  1. Intake & Logging (Within 12 Hours): Inquiries submitted via WhatsApp or email are indexed with subject code, module number, and timestamp.
  2. Academic Cross-Verification (Within 24 Hours): The flagged question or summary is compared against official Calicut University textbooks, standard academic reference works, and peer-reviewed literature.
  3. Production Deployment & Changelog (Within 48 Hours): If an erratum is verified, the fix is deployed directly to the live page and logged in the revision history.
