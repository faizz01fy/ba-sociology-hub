Step 1: Open Your Canva Template

Step 2: Add "Bulk Create" App
On the left toolbar in Canva, click Apps ➔ Search "Bulk Create".
Step 3: Upload the Generated CSV File
Click "Upload data" and select your generated .csv file.
Step 4: Connect Elements & Generate
Right-click each text box in Canva ➔ Click "Connect Data" ➔ Select the corresponding tag shown below:
