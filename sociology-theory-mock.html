Crucial foundational concepts from Calicut University FYUGP syllabus for rapid revision before attempting the mock exam:

1. Origin & Foundations (Pages 1–4)

Coined by Auguste Comte (1839) (Socius + Logos). Comte's Positivism & Law of Three Stages; Durkheim's Social Institutions & Facts; Weber's Interpretive Understanding of Social Action.

2. Industrial Revolution (Pages 5–9)

Agrarian to machine production; massive rural-to-urban migration; breakdown of joint families into wage-earning nuclear families; rise of urban pathology (slums, poverty, unemployment).

3. French Revolution of 1789 (Pages 10–14)

Overthrow of absolute monarchy & 3-Estate feudal system; rise of Liberty, Equality, Fraternity, Secularism & Democracy; post-revolution disorder pushing the quest for social order.

4. Renaissance & Enlightenment (Pages 15–23)

Renaissance humanism (Erasmus), empirical observation & individual dignity; Enlightenment (Age of Reason: Locke, Rousseau, Montesquieu) proving society operates under rational, discoverable laws.

5. Robert Bierstedt's Nature of Sociology (Pages 23–25)

The Social Order (1957): Sociology is an Independent, Social, Categorical (not normative - ethically neutral), Pure (not applied), Abstract, Generalising (not particularising), General (not special), and both Rational & Empirical Science.
