HISTORIOGRAPHY · ചരിത്രരചന 01

Changing Perspectives on Prehistoric Kerala

Traditional Eurocentric and Pan-Indian models considered Kerala an isolated, impassable rainforest devoid of Stone Age settlements. Recent archaeological discoveries along river valleys and mountain passes prove active prehistoric human adaptation.

CHRONOLOGY · കാലഘട്ട വിഭജനം 02

Three-Stage Stone Age Evolution

The Old Stone Age (Palaeolithic / 99% of human history), the Transitional Middle Stone Age (Mesolithic / Microlithic cultures), and the New Stone Age (Neolithic food production revolution) in Kerala exhibit unique regional continuity.

ECOLOGICAL PARADIGM · പരിസ്ഥിതി സിദ്ധാന്തം 03

Geographical Adaptations Across Eco-Zones

Unlike the arid plains of the Deccan, prehistoric communities in Kerala settled across the Sahyadri hill slopes, dense forest borders, and river basins, utilizing locally available quartz and gneiss stones.
