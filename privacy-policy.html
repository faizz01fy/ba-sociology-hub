User Privacy & Data Protection

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Academic Hub ("academichubfaiz.in", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and safeguards your information when you visit and interact with our educational website.

1. Log Files & Technical Browsing Information

Like most standard website operators, Academic Hub follows standard procedures for utilizing log files. When visitors access our website via Cloudflare Edge infrastructure, our servers automatically log certain non-personally identifiable technical information. This includes:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) addresses (used for edge routing, security, and approximate geolocation)
  • Browser type, device classification, and operating system
  • Date, time stamp, referring pages, and requested URLs
  • Approximate geographic area (country, region, city)

This technical data is not linked to any information that is personally identifiable. It is utilized solely for analyzing server traffic trends, administering the site, mitigating DDoS/spam attacks, and optimizing network delivery.

2. Google Analytics & Web Metrics

We use Google Analytics to understand aggregate audience trends, popular modules, and bounce rates. Google Analytics uses cookies to gather data about visitor engagement. You can review Google's Privacy & Terms and learn how Google collects and processes data by visiting How Google uses information from sites that use our services.

You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking across all websites by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

3. Google AdSense & Advertising Cookies

Academic Hub uses or may configure third-party advertising partners, including Google AdSense, to serve advertisements when you visit our website. These companies may use cookies, web beacons, and similar tracking technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other sites across the internet.

  • DoubleClick DART Cookie: Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on Academic Hub. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Personalized Ads Opt-Out: Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting aboutads.info or Your Online Choices.
  • Third-party ad servers or ad networks use technology in their respective advertisements and links that appear on Academic Hub, which are sent directly to users' browsers. They automatically receive your IP address when this occurs. Academic Hub has no access to or control over these cookies that are used by third-party advertisers.

4. Practice Test Results & Student Quizzes

When you complete a practice multiple-choice assessment (MCQ test) and submit your scorecard, you may provide a name or alias for local score reporting. The test submission records:

  • Entered name or alias
  • Subject, module name, score, percentage, and unanswered counts
  • Submission timestamp and automated completion status

This assessment record is utilized strictly for evaluating overall assessment difficulty and displaying test completion statistics. It is never sold, shared with marketing agencies, or linked to advertising identifiers.

5. AI Study Helper & Interactive Tools

Where AI study helper tools are active, user prompts are transmitted to Cloudflare Workers AI solely to generate relevant educational responses. Conversations remain within the active browser session tab and clear upon refreshing or exiting. We advise students never to enter sensitive or personally identifiable information into the AI study tool.

6. Children's Information (COPPA & Digital Safety)

Protecting the privacy of children is especially important to us. Academic Hub is tailored for college and university undergraduate students. We do not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If a parent or guardian believes that Academic Hub has in its database the personally identifiable information of a child under 13, please contact us immediately, and we will use our best efforts to promptly remove such information from our records.

7. Data Protection Rights (GDPR & Global Standards)

Depending on your location, you may have specific data protection rights, including:

  • The right to access: You have the right to request copies of your personal data held by us.
  • The right to rectification: You have the right to request that we correct any information you believe is inaccurate.
  • The right to erasure: You have the right to request that we erase your personal data under certain conditions.

If you make a request, we have one month to respond to you. If you would like to exercise any of these rights, please reach out via our contact channels.

8. External Links

Our website contains links to external sites, including official Calicut University circular portals, PDF reference links, and WhatsApp support. If you click on a third-party link, you will be directed to that site. We strongly advise you to review the Privacy Policy of every site you visit, as we have no control over and assume no responsibility for third-party practices.

9. Contact for Privacy Inquiries

If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, please do not hesitate to contact us at support@academichubfaiz.in or through our Contact Us page.
