K-TET Complete Syllabus Overview (Category I, II, III & IV)

The Kerala Teacher Eligibility Test (K-TET) conducted by Pareeksha Bhavan under SCERT guidelines assesses teaching aptitude, child psychology, language competence, and subject mastery across four qualification categories:

Category I (Lower Primary · Classes 1–5)

Covers Child Development & Pedagogy, Language I (Malayalam/Tamil/Kannada), Language II (English/Arabic), Mathematics, and Environmental Studies (EVS).

Category II (Upper Primary · Classes 6–8)

Assesses Child Development & Pedagogy, Language I, Language II, and optional specialization in Science & Mathematics or Social Science.

Category III (High School · Classes 8–10)

Focuses on Adolescent Psychology, Theories of Learning, Teaching Aptitude, Language I, and High School subject-specific pedagogy.

Category IV (Specialist & Language Teachers)

Evaluates Child Development, Pedagogy & Teacher Aptitude along with Hindi, Arabic, Urdu, Sanskrit, Physical Education, Art & Craft subjects.

Solved SCERT Model Questions & Rectified Key Explanations

Model Q1 · Category I Child Development & Pedagogy

Which of the following developmental periods is generally termed as 'the period of conflicts and turmoil' in human growth?

  • A. Infancy
  • B. Childhood
  • C. Adolescence
  • D. Adulthood
Verified Answer: (C) Adolescence

Explanation: Adolescence is widely designated as a period of 'stress and storm' or conflict (as noted by Stanley Hall) due to rapid biological, emotional, and social changes.

Model Q2 · Category I Child Development & Pedagogy

Assume a student is provided a list of correlated words to study and asked to recall them at a later time. According to J.P. Guilford's Structure of Intellect (SI) model, the content dimension of this test is:

  • A. Symbolic
  • B. Behavioural
  • C. Figural
  • D. Semantic
Verified Answer: (D) Semantic

Explanation: In Guilford's Structure of Intellect model, verbal ideas, words, and meanings belong to the 'Semantic' content dimension.

Model Q3 · Category I Child Development & Pedagogy

In order to help an intellectually challenged child in an inclusive classroom, which pedagogical strategy is most constructive?

  • A. Send him immediately to a separate segregated institution
  • B. Do not provide any special attention so as not to single him out
  • C. Provide him with simple, concrete learning experiences and scaffolded tasks
  • D. Ask him to independently change his learning style
Verified Answer: (C) Provide him with simple, concrete learning experiences and scaffolded tasks

Explanation: Inclusive education emphasizes providing differentiated instruction with concrete learning aids, step-by-step scaffolding, and hands-on experiences.

Model Q4 · Category I Child Development & Pedagogy

A child in your classroom constantly attributes his academic failures to other children or external circumstances. Which psychological defense mechanism is being exhibited?

  • A. Rationalization
  • B. Projection
  • C. Repression
  • D. Compensation
Verified Answer: (B) Projection

Explanation: Projection is the ego-defense mechanism wherein an individual projects their own inadequacies, mistakes, or impulses onto other people or external conditions.

Model Q5 · Category I Environmental Studies (EVS)

Which of the following is considered an abiotic (non-living) component of a natural terrestrial ecosystem?

  • A. Decomposers (bacteria and fungi)
  • B. Primary producers (green plants)
  • C. Soil minerals and moisture
  • D. Herbivores
Verified Answer: (C) Soil minerals and moisture

Explanation: Abiotic factors consist of physical and chemical elements such as sunlight, air, water, temperature, and soil minerals, whereas organisms are biotic.

Model Q6 · Category II Science & Mathematics Pedagogy

In learning mathematics, Bruner's sequence of cognitive representation progresses in which order?

  • A. Iconic → Enactive → Symbolic
  • B. Enactive → Iconic → Symbolic
  • C. Symbolic → Iconic → Enactive
  • D. Enactive → Symbolic → Iconic
Verified Answer: (B) Enactive → Iconic → Symbolic

Explanation: Jerome Bruner proposed that learning advances from Enactive (action-based / concrete objects) to Iconic (image-based) to Symbolic (language and mathematical symbols).

Model Q7 · Category II Social Science

The Right of Children to Free and Compulsory Education (RTE) Act in India was enacted by Parliament in which year?

  • A. 2002
  • B. 2005
  • C. 2009
  • D. 2012
Verified Answer: (C) 2009

Explanation: The RTE Act was enacted in August 2009 under Article 21A of the Constitution of India and came into full effect on April 1, 2010.

Model Q8 · Category III Adolescent Psychology & Learning Theories

According to Jean Piaget's stages of cognitive development, the ability to formulate hypotheses and execute abstract deductive reasoning emerges during which stage?

  • A. Sensorimotor stage
  • B. Pre-operational stage
  • C. Concrete operational stage
  • D. Formal operational stage
Verified Answer: (D) Formal operational stage

Explanation: The Formal Operational Stage (typically beginning around age 11-12 into adolescence) allows hypothetical-deductive reasoning, abstract conceptualization, and systematic problem testing.

K-TET, Category III High School Psychology & Learning Theories
Model Q9 · Category III Subject Pedagogy

Formative Assessment is primarily designed for:

  • A. Grading learners at the end of the academic year
  • B. Diagnosing learning gaps and providing ongoing pedagogical feedback during instruction
  • C. Comparing schools for national accreditation
  • D. Selecting students for competitive merit scholarships
Verified Answer: (B) Diagnosing learning gaps and providing ongoing pedagogical feedback during instruction

Explanation: Formative evaluation occurs during the instructional process to monitor student learning and adjust teaching strategies, distinguishing it from summative evaluation.

K-TET, Category III Teaching Aptitude & Assessment
Model Q10 · Category IV Specialist & Language Pedagogy

Which approach in second-language teaching places maximum focus on communicative competence rather than rote mastery of grammatical drills?

  • A. Grammar-Translation Method
  • B. Audio-Lingual Drill Method
  • C. Communicative Language Teaching (CLT)
  • D. Direct Structural Mimicry
Verified Answer: (C) Communicative Language Teaching (CLT)

Explanation: CLT (Communicative Language Teaching) prioritizes functional, meaningful communication in real-life contexts over mechanical grammatical drills.
