KTET Test

A separate revision guide for choosing the right K-TET category, following the current syllabus, and using official model questions and answer keys.

Independent study guide. Academic Hub is not Pareeksha Bhavan or an official K-TET website. Always confirm eligibility, dates, and exam instructions from the latest official notification.

Choose your category from the current notification

The official K-TET site publishes separate information for Categories I, II, III, and IV. The correct category depends on the teaching post and the candidate’s qualifications. Do not choose a category from an old question paper alone: check the latest notification and category-specific qualification details first.

Then open the matching syllabus and model questions. The official site describes K-TET as covering Lower Primary, Upper Primary, and High School classes, and its Category IV qualification information covers language teachers, specialist teachers, and Physical Education teachers. Use the current notification to confirm which route applies to you.

A category-aware practice routine

  1. Write your target category and the qualification route you are checking.
  2. Read the latest notification, then download that category’s current syllabus from the official site.
  3. Use model questions to learn the paper style. Treat older examples as practice, not proof of the current pattern.
  4. After a timed attempt, compare your responses with the official key for the same exam, category, and paper code.
  5. Group mistakes by syllabus topic and practise those topics again before taking another full paper.

Check which answer key is current

K-TET announcements may include provisional and later rectified answer keys. Confirm that the category and question booklet match your paper, and check the announcement date and status before scoring your attempt. If a later key replaces an earlier one, update your error log rather than keeping the old score as final.
